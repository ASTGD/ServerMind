Whole jobs, not commands

It does the whole job — not just one command

Ask Ally to host a WordPress site or clean up a hacked server and it runs the entire job: plan a step, do it, check the result, decide the next one. It won't tell you "done" until it has proof the job actually worked.

Safety built in

Safe by design — and un-trickable

Ally blocks dangerous commands, asks before anything it can't undo, and verifies its own work. It treats everything it reads on your server as information, not orders — so even a compromised server can't trick it into running malware. Your credentials are encrypted and never shown back.
