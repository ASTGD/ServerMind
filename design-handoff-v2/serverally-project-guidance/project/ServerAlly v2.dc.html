03 · Safe by design

It guards your servers — and can't be tricked

Scheduled scans watch for signs of a hack — webshells, miners, rogue cron jobs, backdoor accounts — and alert you the moment a server worsens. Cleanup is always a careful, evidence-preserving mission that waits for your approval.

  • Dangerous commands blocked; risky ones wait for your OK
  • Everything read on a server is data, not instructions — a hacked box can't trick Ally (validated with live red-team tests)
  • Credentials AES-256-GCM encrypted, never shown back
