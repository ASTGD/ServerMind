# 00 — Overview & Positioning

## One-sentence positioning
**ServerAlly is an AI companion that lets anyone manage, automate, and secure any server — Linux, Windows, hosting panels, or cloud — by describing what they want in plain English.**

## The core problem we solve
Owning a server and *running* one are two very different things. Founders, agencies, and small
businesses end up with a VPS, a cPanel account, or a cloud instance they depend on — but the
moment something needs doing (install WordPress, renew an SSL cert, the site is down, a security
scan), they hit a wall of SSH, cryptic commands, and man pages. Their choices today are: learn
system administration, pay a freelancer for every small task, or cross their fingers.

ServerAlly removes that wall. You type what you want ("my site is showing a white screen, fix
it") and Ally — the AI companion — figures out the safe steps, runs them with your approval,
watches the output, and explains what happened in plain language. No terminal knowledge required.

**Who feels this most:** non-technical owners of production infrastructure who can't afford
downtime but can't (or don't want to) become sysadmins.

## Primary target audience
- **Roles:** founders, indie hackers, designers, agency owners, bloggers, small-business owners,
  operations people who "inherited" the server.
- **Company size:** solo → small teams (1–20 people). Agencies/MSPs managing many client servers
  are a strong secondary segment (fleet + team features).
- **Technical level:** low to intermediate. They can click around a control panel and copy-paste
  a command if forced to — but they are not sysadmins and don't want to be.

## Top competitors & how we're different
| Competitor | What they are | How ServerAlly is different |
|---|---|---|
| **RunCloud / Ploi / ServerPilot** | Server control panels for developers (Linux/PHP focus). | They still assume you understand servers and are **Linux-only, no AI**. ServerAlly is **AI-native** (describe it in plain English) and works across **Linux, Windows, hosting panels, and cloud** — not just Linux LAMP/LEMP. |
| **cPanel / Plesk** | Traditional hosting control panels. | Dated UI, menu-maze, **no AI**, and tied to one server's stack. ServerAlly *manages* cPanel/Plesk boxes too, and adds a plain-English layer and cross-server automation on top. |
| **Doing it yourself (SSH + ChatGPT)** | Copy-pasting commands from a chatbot into a terminal. | A generic chatbot can't *see* your server, can't run anything, and has no safety net. ServerAlly connects to the real box, runs steps with **approval + safety checks**, **verifies** the result, and **resists prompt-injection** from compromised servers. |

**The one-line wedge:** competitors give technical people a nicer control panel; ServerAlly gives
**non-technical people an expert** who does the work for them, safely, on any kind of server.

## Tone of voice
**Calm · reassuring · plain-spoken · competent · friendly (never dumbed-down).**

We sound like a trusted expert friend who happens to run servers for a living — never
condescending, never jargon-heavy, never hype-y. We're honest about risk and always explain
in human terms.

Example sentences in that voice:
- "Tell Ally what you want in your own words. It handles the commands — and explains what it did."
- "Ally asks before anything risky, checks its own work, and never runs a command it can't undo without telling you first."
- "Your server, any kind — a cloud VPS, a Windows box, a cPanel account. One companion for all of them."
