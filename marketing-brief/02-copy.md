## How it works (3 steps)

1. **Connect your server.** Add a Linux box, a Windows server, a cPanel/CyberPanel account, or a
   whole cloud account. Your login is encrypted — Ally connects, you never touch a config file.
2. **Just ask.** Type what you want ("install WordPress," "my site is down," "set up nightly
   backups"). Ally reads the server, proposes a plan, and shows you before it runs anything.
3. **Approve & relax.** Ally does the work step-by-step, checks that it actually worked, and
   explains it in plain English. Risky steps always wait for your OK.

## Feature sections

### 1. Talk to your server like a person
Forget SSH, man pages, and Stack Overflow. Ally understands "why is my site slow?" the same way a
sysadmin would — it looks at the real server, finds the cause, and fixes it with your approval.
Answers in your language (8 supported).

### 2. It does the whole job — not just one command
Ask Ally to *host a WordPress site* or *clean up a hacked server* and it runs the entire job:
plan a step, do it, check the result, decide the next one. It won't tell you "done" until it has
**proof** the job actually worked.

### 3. Safe by design — and un-trickable
Ally blocks dangerous commands, asks before anything it can't undo, and verifies its own work.
It treats everything it reads on your server as information, not orders — so even a compromised
server can't trick it into running malware. Your credentials are encrypted and never shown back.

### 4. It watches your servers so you don't have to
ServerAlly quietly checks your fleet for health problems and signs of a hack, scores each server,
and emails you what needs attention — with a one-click fix. You hear about the disk filling up
*before* the site goes down.

### 5. One companion for every kind of server
Linux VPS, bare metal, Windows Server, cPanel / CyberPanel / Plesk hosting, or an entire AWS,
DigitalOcean, Hetzner, Google Cloud, or Azure account. Add them all to one dashboard and let Ally
manage them together.

## FAQ

**Do I need to know Linux or the command line?**
No. That's the whole point. You describe what you want in plain English and Ally handles the
commands. It explains everything it does in language anyone can follow.

**Is it safe to let an AI run commands on my server?**
Yes — safety is built in, not bolted on. Ally blocks destructive commands, asks for your approval
before anything risky, and verifies its own work before finishing. It also treats anything it
reads on your server as data, not instructions, so a hacked server can't trick it.

**What kinds of servers does it work with?**
Linux servers (Ubuntu, Debian, AlmaLinux, and more) over SSH, Windows Server over WinRM, hosting
panels (CyberPanel, cPanel, Plesk, DirectAdmin), and cloud accounts (AWS, DigitalOcean, Hetzner,
Google Cloud, Azure).

**How does Ally connect to my server?**
You add your server once with its login details, which are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before they're
stored. Ally connects over the standard secure protocols (SSH / WinRM / the panel's own API). Your
credentials are never shown back to you or anyone else after you save them.

**Can my team use it too?**
Yes. Invite teammates and give each person access to specific servers with a role (view, operate,
or admin). People with view-only access can never run commands.
